PASSPORT / LOCALNET EXPERIMENT

Use your Ledger as a grant key.

A fresh account receives 100 test units. Its owner grants your Ledger authority to spend up to 12 units, with a maximum of 10 per call and one fixed recipient.

CONNECTING

Connecting…

Connect and unlock your Flex over USB, then open Ledger Security Key.


Import uses only the public key and credential ID. A new signature is required for each account request. For a new credential, choose a USB security key in the browser prompt.

What happens

  1. Approve a 5-unit spend from the grant.
  2. Sign a 1-unit revocation test. The owner revokes the grant before this request is attempted, so it must fail.

Only non-native localnet test assets are used. The software test owner pays fees and manages the grant. Ledger WebAuthn confirms the localhost relying party; the operation details are displayed by this browser, not on the Ledger screen.
