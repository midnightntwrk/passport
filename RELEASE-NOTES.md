Build notes for the NEXT production deploy. Rewrite this file before every deploy; `scripts/tag-release.mjs` refuses to tag without a "## Fixed" section.

## Fixed
- **A passkey with no PRF extension was refused with a false diagnosis and no way out** (found by the Android-shape suite, 2026/09/04). On an Android or Google Password Manager combination that implements neither largeBlob nor PRF, enrolment made the passkey, found it could derive no key, and the landing screen said "Passport passkeys require a valid HTTPS origin or localhost relying-party domain" — on localhost, about an origin that was fine, as a bare banner with nothing under it and both remaining buttons leading back to the same failure. The sentence was an accident: the refusal ended "…or PRF-capable security key", and the backend's message mapping decided by substring over the text and matched the word "security". The refusal now travels as itself rather than as a string, the mapping decides by the error's name instead of its wording, and the screen says "This passkey cannot be used for Passport on this device — try a different passkey or device." with "Use a different passkey" beneath it — the door that reaches a passkey held on a phone. It deliberately does not offer "Create a new passkey": the passkey this device makes is the thing that failed, so a second one is the same passkey.
- **Android: stuck with an orphan passkey; a new passkey kept showing the old failed profile; "The passkey prompt did not finish"** (reported by Hector, 2026/09/04). Three causes: the name record was keyed by network rather than by passkey, so any passkey inherited it; the app asked Android's platform passkeys (Google Password Manager) for the largeBlob extension they do not support, which narrows Chrome's account sheet to nothing and the prompt never settles; and there was no way to start clean. Now: every local record is bound to the passkey that owns it and handed back only to a passkey that can be shown to own the account; no largeBlob request is made to a passkey without it; "I already have a name — find my Passport" restores an account by its .night name, verified on chain against the passkey (one prompt), which is the only door on Android since there is no blob there; "Set up a new Passport on this device" forgets this device's records and starts the create path without touching the passkey or the chain. Also fixed while reproducing: sign-out during the silent restore was undone by it, and a legacy record survived the way out. (319eb78)
- **Releases did not say what they fixed** (Hector, 2026/09/04). The tag step now refuses to run without these notes. (4a2e599, 6932d99)
- **A refused registration still spent a grant coin** (found by the 2026/09/04 sponsor soak). A signup refused its name at the sponsor's hourly ceiling at 17:38:02 was funded a second later anyway, and ended holding NIGHT and mUSD with no name against it — a grant that is once per account, ever, spent on a Passport nobody can send to by name. The opening balance is fired the moment the account lands rather than after the name answers, so the two race and both orders happen; a refusal that leaves the name queued now holds the account's grant, is read before every attempt in the schedule rather than only at the call site, and is lifted where a name registers — which is the queued name's "Register now" as well as onboarding, since both run the one claim.
- **A ceiling refusal told the reader the sponsor was busy and their name would register on its own** (found by the 2026/09/04 sponsor soak). Neither half was true: the sponsor was answering in 60 ms with four spend lanes open and refused in 25 ms without attempting anything, and nothing was ever going to register that name — the only thing that will is somebody pressing Register now. A reader who believed it waited for something that could not happen. A refusal because too many names have been registered lately now says so and says what to do, with the Try again and Continue to Home controls unchanged; the busy sentence is kept for the refusals that genuinely mean a spend of the sponsor's own is in the way.
- **"Your account is ready" beside a balance of mUSD 0** (found by the 2026/09/04 stability audit). The opening balance is two deposits and they do not arrive together, but the "On the way" row retired the moment the account held anything at all — so a new Passport lost the row as the NIGHT landed and then sat for 18.1 seconds showing mUSD 0 with nothing on screen saying the stablecoin was still coming. The row now waits for BOTH halves of the grant. A refusal or an exhausted schedule still ends the wait exactly as before, including when one half did land, and a build whose sponsor names no stablecoin colour is unaffected. (8388fdb)
- **A busy sponsor was passed over instead of waited for** (found by the 2026/09/04 stability audit). The balancer answers `429 PENDING_TRANSACTION` with a `retryAfterMs` when it is already balancing something — a queue position, not a refusal — and the client ignored the figure and posted the same transaction to the 1AM gateway instead, throwing away a sponsor that was two seconds from saying yes and building the leg elsewhere against a contract state the first one was already moving. The hint is now honoured where it was given: up to three waits on the same endpoint, for as long as the sponsor asked inside the existing clamp, drawn from the caller's existing window so the total wait is unchanged. Still busy after that and the fall-through happens as before. Every other retryable refusal is untouched. (2119201)
- **A payment interrupted by a reload sat waiting to be pressed** (found by the 2026/09/04 stability audit). A reload 33 seconds into a mUSD send left the recovery card on Home for three minutes doing nothing; pressing "Continue" then finished the payment exactly as it would have without the reload. The money was at the sender's own Passport the whole time and every step left was one this device could take on its own, so the wait bought nothing and cost the recipient three minutes. A payment whose first step has landed is one the sender already authorised, so Home now carries it on by itself a few seconds after the session is ready, and the card says it is working. "Continue" stays for the two cases that need a person: a payment that has not moved anything yet, and one that stopped with a reason the reader is entitled to finish reading. One automatic attempt per payment per session. (69e4006)
- **The recovery card would not say which asset was in flight** (found by the 2026/09/04 stability audit). It read "SENDING 1 UNITS TO SBMTN702YEJFH.NIGHT" over a payment of one mUSD. "Units" names nothing, and the one surface that appears when something has gone wrong was the only one not saying it — the Send sheet that opened the payment and the balance above the card both said mUSD. The card now names the asset and puts the amount on that asset's own scale, from the same source both balance lists are painted from, so all three surfaces agree. (46ed7f5)
- **The sponsor turned people away an hour before it had to, and `/status` did not say so** (found by the 2026/09/04 sponsor soak). The hourly ceilings are global rather than per client, and 18 signups sharing the service with one other tester exhausted both — about 20 people an hour across everybody, with two demos running at once halving each other. Nothing else in that hour was near its limit: spend lanes never fell below 3 after the first burst, DUST recovered to 90% of its start within 16–56 s of every burst, and every one of the 30 grant coins spent was a distinct UTxO. The deployed ceilings are now 60 registrations and 80 funded accounts an hour, with the per-client limits deliberately unchanged. Ceiling refusals are also counted now: `refusedRateLimited` read 0 through six genuine 429s because the ceilings refuse in the route handlers and never touched the guard's counter, and `/status` carries `refusedCeiling` beside it — the half only a bigger ceiling would fix.
