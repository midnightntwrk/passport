ARC · Midnight Passport

An account that is a name, not a seed phrase

Midnight Passport is the identity and account layer for the Midnight network. You are alice.passport.night: created with a passkey instead of a seed phrase, usable from any of your devices, recoverable after losing all of them, and held by a contract that only your proofs can move. This site shows how the system works and exactly where the work stands.

Today

Four things that are true right now

Published

The account standards are upstream

MIP-0012 (contract custody) and MIP-0013 (multi-key authorisation) are published in the Midnight improvement-proposal repository as Proposed, with a reference implementation whose conformance suites pass.

Standards → Validated

Zero-token onboarding works

A user holding no tokens at all had their account contract deployed and their first calls paid by a sponsor, end to end on a production node. No airdrop prerequisite exists.

The fee canvas → Validated

Losing every device is survivable

Recovery uses guardians who store nothing between ceremonies, running in the working prototype. One transaction retires every old device and grant; the account, name, and balances never move.

How recovery works → In delivery

Status

Where the work stands

One vocabulary throughout: a component is specified when a published standard covers it, decided when the choice is set in stone but the specification is pending, an open decision when the design space is still live, and in design otherwise. Standards are counted separately from components.

The evidence trail

Newest first; every entry is backed by an artefact — an experiment against a real node, a merge in the upstream proposal repository, or a decision record. Component chips open the canvas in place.

      What we need from the ecosystem

      Adoption is the point: a standard nobody else implements is shelfware. These are the four concrete things the wider ecosystem can do next.

      Midnight Foundation

      Acquire passport.night

      The decided name shape (alice.passport.night) hangs on the Foundation holding the parent domain in the upstream name service and offering sub-domains free, first come, first served.

      Everyone

      Review MIP-0012 and MIP-0013

      Both keystone standards are Proposed upstream and open for comment. Implementation errata are already folded in; ecosystem review is what moves them toward acceptance.

      One body of work, two surfaces

      Feature-complete v1.0 is a body of deliverables: ratified MIPs, cryptographer and formal-methods sign-off, and evidence.
