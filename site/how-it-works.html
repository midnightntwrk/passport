How a Passport account works

One account is one contract on the Midnight ledger. The storage split

What lives where

The end state of the simulation, written down. The on-chain column is the account's entire public footprint; the off-chain column is every piece of private material and who holds it. No column contains a seed phrase, and no row requires an operator.

On-chain — public, permanent

  • Device commitments and the epoch each was enrolled in. Hashes: they recognise a device, they do not identify it.devices · device_epoch
  • Unshielded balances held by the contract (Night), public by design.night_balances
  • Shielded holdings of the contract, stored as sealed commitments with an encrypted inbox for the owner: amount and token type are not readable on-chain. The older vault recipe exposed both; the standard (MIP-0012) records the private pattern.coins
  • Grant scopes: commitment, token color, cumulative cap, spend so far, active flag. The scope is enforced by the circuit, so it must be readable by it.grants
  • Recovery commitment: one hash of the current recovery secret.recovery
  • The BUSS public vector φ: two to four field points that provably leak nothing about the secret, plus the session nonce. Ninety-six bytes covers a three-of-five setup.recovery_phi · recovery_session
  • The round counter: replay defence, bumped by every authorised call.round

Off-chain — private, distributed

  • Device secrets, one per device, derived from each passkey on every use and never stored.your devices
  • The recovery secret, held transiently by your wallet; reconstructable from a guardian quorum plus the public φ.your wallet
  • Guardian keys: each guardian's own long-lived key, which they already had. Guardians store nothing for you.each guardian
  • Paper keys: random keys written down; each slip is a guardian.wherever you put them
  • The guardian roster: who they are, how many, and the threshold. Deliberately not on-chain, so the ledger reveals nothing about your social graph.your memory or a note
  • Grant secrets, held by the dApp they were issued to.each dApp
Findings this page records

What we validated to be able to show this

Every mechanism in the simulation above runs for real in the account-custody prototype against a local Midnight network: same circuits, same storage layout, same recovery mathematics. The pattern has since graduated: the reference implementation at contract/ realises the published standards (MIP-0012 and MIP-0013) in one deployment, with the conformance suites both standards require passing.

Recovery · C14 / C15

Community recovery without guardian storage

Recovery adopts BUSS, the bottom-up secret sharing construction from the ANARKey paper. Guardians derive their share on demand from a key they already hold and store nothing between ceremonies; paper keys join people under one threshold. The on-chain footprint is a commitment plus a few public points, and the previous placeholder (plaintext shares in public state) is removed rather than patched. Two rules are load-bearing: a fresh session and a fresh secret for every published backup, and a full re-ceremony on any guardian change.

Custody · C1 / C4

Private contract custody of shielded assets is possible

The belief that contract-held shielded coins must show their amounts and types in public state was proven wrong on a real node. The publicity came from the widely copied vault recipe, which also silently lost change returned to the contract, a defect found and fixed along the way. The validated stateless pattern keeps holdings as sealed commitments with an encrypted inbox, so an observer learns nothing while the owner can always rebuild the coin store. The per-account contract custodies Night and shielded tokens end to end this way: permissionless deposits, device-authorised and grant-authorised withdrawals, and partial spends with change.

Keys · C5 / C9

Keys validate operations through one seam

Every authorised circuit performs the same check: the caller proves knowledge of a secret whose commitment is enrolled at the current device epoch. Secrets never leave the device; only proofs cross. The check lives in a single helper circuit — the seam — and the replacement it was shaped for has happened: the reference implementation instantiates the seam with in-circuit JubJub Schnorr per MIP-0013, without touching any other circuit. One epoch bump retires every device and grant at once, which is exactly what recovery relies on.
